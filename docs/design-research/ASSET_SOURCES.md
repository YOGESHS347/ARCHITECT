## 1. Illustration Provenance
All architectural illustrations, diagrams, and drawing compositions implemented in this redesign are **100% original, bespoke SVG/CSS vector creations** developed specifically for this project:

1. **The Initial Concept Sketch (`ConceptSketchArtwork.tsx`):**
   - Original digital drawing in fine graphite strokes, exploratory construction guidelines, human-style annotations, and delicate ground lines.
   - Author: Antigravity Creative Engineering Studio.
   - License: MIT / Proprietary to AI Architect.

2. **The Measured Architectural Floor Plan (`MeasuredPlanArtwork.tsx`):**
   - Original vector drafting adhering to standard architectural CAD conventions: structural double walls, partition lines, 90° door swings with arc paths, triple-glazed window indicators, CAD extension strings, 45° tick marks, north indicator, and 1:50 scale bar.
   - Author: Antigravity Creative Engineering Studio.
   - License: MIT / Proprietary to AI Architect.

3. **The Exploded Multi-Floor Axonometric (`ExplodedAxoArtwork.tsx`):**
   - Original 3-level isometric drawing displaying Ground Floor, First Floor, and Roof Terrace separated vertically and connected through the continuous vertical circulation staircase core.
   - Author: Antigravity Creative Engineering Studio.
   - License: MIT / Proprietary to AI Architect.

4. **The Architectural Building Section (`BuildingSectionArtwork.tsx`):**
   - Original sectional cutaway illustration illustrating foundation ground line, floor slabs, ceiling heights, vertical stair treads, daylight angles, and architectural annotations.
   - Author: Antigravity Creative Engineering Studio.
   - License: MIT / Proprietary to AI Architect.

5. **The Material and Design Study Board (`MaterialStudyArtwork.tsx`):**
   - Art-directed architectural mood board featuring procedural material swatches (board-formed concrete, warm rift oak, fluted glass, brushed bronze) and lighting studies.
   - Author: Antigravity Creative Engineering Studio.
   - License: MIT / Proprietary to AI Architect.
